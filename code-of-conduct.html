Code of Conduct

Swift Ahmedabad Community Guidelines

Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and organizers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected Behavior

Be welcoming, inclusive, and respectful. Use kind and considerate language. Be collaborative and supportive of fellow community members. Respect differing viewpoints and experiences. Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.

Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, trolling, public or private intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, and any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by emailing helloswiftahmedabad@gmail.com. All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

Enforcement

Community organizers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions and to ban temporarily or permanently any participant for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
