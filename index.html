A LITTLE OF AHMEDABAD

Explore Ahmedabad

Hover or tap a landmark to discover its story.

The illustration couldn’t load. Ahmedabad’s stories are still here.

  • Old-city pols — Traditional neighbourhoods bring closely grouped homes, carved facades and shared community spaces together.
  • Sidi Saiyyed jaali — The Tree of Life lattice turns carved stone into a delicate pattern of intertwining branches.
  • Teen Darwaza — Three grand arches mark one of Ahmedabad’s historic gateways.
  • The chabutra — These bird-feeding towers express the old city’s tradition of caring for its birds.
  • Narendra Modi Stadium — Motera’s cricket stadium brings Ahmedabad together around the game.
  • Sabarmati Ashram — Hriday Kunj was Gandhi’s home within the Sabarmati Ashram.
  • Adalaj Stepwell — Near Ahmedabad, carved galleries and descending stairs lead into Adalaj’s historic stepwell.
  • Atal Bridge — A pedestrian bridge connects the two banks of the Sabarmati River.
